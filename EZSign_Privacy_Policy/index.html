EZSign

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EZSign, a Chrome extension by The Silly Geese, handles information when you use the extension.

Overview

EZSign helps identify Gmail messages and attachments that may need signatures so you can review them more easily. The extension is designed to keep processing lightweight and user-directed.

Information EZSign May Access

Depending on the permissions you grant, EZSign may access limited Gmail account information, message metadata, message search results, and attachment information needed to identify documents that may require signatures.

EZSign does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the document-search and signature-workflow features you choose to use.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed by EZSign is used to:

  • Search for Gmail messages that may contain documents requiring signatures.
  • Display relevant messages and attachments for your review.
  • Maintain a local signing queue or workflow status inside the extension.
  • Improve reliability, security, and functionality of the extension.

Data Sharing

EZSign does not sell your data. EZSign does not share Gmail data with third parties for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

If EZSign later connects to a signing service or backend, files and related information will only be sent when you choose to start that signing workflow.

Data Storage

EZSign may store limited workflow information locally in your browser, such as selected documents or queue status. Local browser storage can be cleared by uninstalling the extension or clearing Chrome extension data.

Google User Data

EZSign’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Choices

You can revoke EZSign’s access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account permissions page. You can also uninstall the Chrome extension from Chrome to stop extension activity.

Children’s Privacy

EZSign is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions about EZSign, contact The Silly Geese through the project owner or repository maintainer.
